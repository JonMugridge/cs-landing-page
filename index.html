Worth remembering

The points from across the training that are easiest to get wrong.

  • Settings are globalAny change in Settings applies to the whole portal you're in, for every user and every invite, not just for you.Go to Settings
  • The Branding card isn't where branding is set upSettings, then Branding, only changes a portal's name and logo. A client's full branding is managed in the dedicated Branding module.Go to Settings
  • Tell Credas when a portal is renamedClients can rename their portal, but if they don't tell us the new name, we can't easily find it when they need help.Go to Settings
  • Ongoing monitoring covers every checkOnce OGM is on, it applies automatically to every Sanctions and PEPs check in that portal, whether stand-alone or part of a journey.Go to Settings
  • Display data needs a signed waiverNever switch on Display data on a portal's Details tab until the client has signed a waiver.Go to Back Office
  • Deletion can't be undoneOnce someone is deleted by a deletion rule, their details can't be viewed or retrieved. Check every period before saving.Go to Settings
  • Approval is a rubber stampApproving or rejecting someone records a decision but doesn't change any of their check results. Add the Approval Status column through Table Configuration to see it.Go to Individuals
  • Every change in remediation is recordedEach status update, with its comment, is listed under Notes above the original automated results, giving a full audit trail.Go to Remediation
  • One role per userRoles are permissions templates. The Roles list allows more than one, but a user should only ever have one role applied. Untick the old one when you change it.Go to Admin Portal
  • Child portals need a Reseller portalOnly a portal set up as a Reseller has the Child portals option. Standard and Reseller Child portals don't.Go to Admin Portal
  • Credas Plus returns PASS or FAILWith Credas Plus switched on, Credas's trained team remediates ID and AML checks that need further action, using set procedures. It doesn't cover adverse media, law enforcement, profiles of interest or disqualified directors.Go to Back Office
  • User setup depends on the clientRoles, user groups, Can see own invites only and No contact user can be combined in different ways. There's no single right setup.Go to Admin Portal
